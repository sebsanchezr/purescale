# PureScale VSL Script — /ads Landing (Cold Paid, $97 SLO)

**Target runtime:** 2.5-3.5 min (cold paid + low-ticket impulse = short and direct, per vsl-architect skill)
**Demographic:** cold ecom operators/founders, interrupted on the feed, zero prior intent
**Offer:** $97 for 10 ad creatives in 24 hours (self-liquidating front end; retainer is the back end)
**Style:** Talking head (Seb), heavily edited — captions, jump cuts, fast pace, proof screenshots, sample-creative b-roll
**Placement:** /ads landing, above the checkout/buy button. Show button + play bar. Isolate page to headline + VSL + buy.
**Congruency:** the ad hook, the page headline, and this VSL open must all say the SAME thing (10 creatives, 24hrs, $97).

Player: Wistia or Vidalytics. 5 chapter markers below.

Note on cold paid: they did not search for you. The first 5 seconds carry the whole thing. Open on payoff, not backstory.

---

## CHAPTER 1 — Summary / pattern interrupt (0:00-0:45)
*Cold traffic. Say the entire offer in the first 45 seconds. They decide fast.*

**Spoken:**
"If you run ecom ads, here's a deal. We'll make you ten scroll-stopping ad creatives in the next twenty-four hours for ninety-seven dollars.

Not a subscription. Not a call. Ten creatives, one day, ninety-seven bucks, built specifically to beat what you're running now.

We've made creatives for brands like Revice Denim and Go Forth Goods and driven over six hundred million in ecom revenue. Now you can test the exact same production for less than the price of one freelancer asset.

I'll show you what you get and why it's this cheap. Or just hit the button and let's go."

**On-screen:** Seb to camera, fast energy. Big caption: "10 AD CREATIVES. 24 HOURS. $97." Flash 3-4 sample creatives. Logos: Revice, Go Forth. Lower-third: "Seb, Founder, PureScale".

---

## CHAPTER 2 — Why creative is the whole game (0:45-1:20)
*Agitate the pain fast. It's not budget or targeting, it's creative.*

**Spoken:**
"Real quick, why does this matter. Your targeting is fine. Meta's algorithm is smarter than all of us. The thing actually capping your ROAS is creative.

You run the same three or four ads for weeks, they fatigue, your cost per purchase quietly climbs, and you feel it too late.

The brands winning right now aren't outspending you. They're out-testing you — feeding the algorithm fresh creative faster than it can burn out. That's the entire game now."

**On-screen:** one tired ad vs a grid of fresh ones. Caption: "You're not outspent. You're out-tested." CAC line drifting up.

---

## CHAPTER 3 — What you get for $97 (1:20-2:05)
*The deliverable. Concrete. Remove risk.*

**Spoken:**
"So here's exactly what ninety-seven dollars gets you.

Ten ad creatives, made for your brand, using your products — not generic templates. A mix of UGC-style, statics, and hooks, each built to test a different angle.

Delivered in twenty-four hours, ready to upload straight into your ad account. You own them outright, run them wherever you want.

All we need from you is a link to your store and your current best ad. That's it. We handle the rest."

**On-screen:** 3-step graphic (send store + best ad → we build 10 in 24hrs → you upload + run). Show real sample creatives on screen. Caption: "You keep everything. Upload and run."

---

## CHAPTER 4 — Proof (2:05-2:35)
*Fast proof. One line each.*

**Spoken:**
"And this actually works.

Revice Denim, nine million to twenty-six million in revenue. Go Forth Goods, ninety-five K to two hundred and forty K a month. Posh, ten X in ninety days. L'alingi, a five X return across two markets at once.

Same creative engine. Now you can run a batch of it for ninety-seven dollars."

**On-screen:** stat cards per brand, matching the landing + email numbers. *(HOLD until Seb confirms every figure.)*

---

## CHAPTER 5 — Close + CTA (2:35-3:00)
*Direct. Low-friction. Urgency without gimmick.*

**Spoken:**
"Here's the deal again. Ten ad creatives, twenty-four hours, ninety-seven dollars, built to beat your current ads.

Worst case, you spent ninety-seven bucks and got ten fresh assets to test. Best case, one of them becomes your new winner and pays for itself on day one.

Hit the button below, send us your store, and we'll have your ten creatives back to you tomorrow. Let's go."

**On-screen:** arrow to buy button. Caption: "Get 10 creatives in 24hrs — $97." Trust line: "Own everything. Delivered in 24 hours."

## Delivery + edit checklist
- [ ] Runtime 2.5-3.5 min. Cold + low-ticket = shorter is better. Cut anything slow.
- [ ] First 5 seconds = the offer, on screen and spoken. No intro, no backstory.
- [ ] First 45s stands alone (offer + price + proof + CTA all named).
- [ ] Ad hook → page headline → VSL open all say the SAME thing (10 / 24hrs / $97). Congruency or the click dies.
- [ ] Player = Wistia/Vidalytics, 5 chapter markers, button + play bar visible.
- [ ] Thumbnail demonstrates payoff: "10 ad creatives in 24hrs for $97." Test clip vs static (page speed).
- [ ] Captions on, fast jump cuts, real sample creatives on screen, no dead air.
- [ ] Page isolated: headline + VSL + buy button only. Nothing competing.
- [ ] Numbers on screen match everywhere else. HOLD until Seb signs off figures.
- [ ] Post-purchase: upsell page / email teeing up the retainer (this is where the real ROI is).
